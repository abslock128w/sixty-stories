# Owner's production directive: hero asset quality benchmark (2026-11-17)

Change of approach. Stop broad, generic improvements to the whole city. Preserve the
existing layout, lighting, composition and architecture. First establish a much higher
standard of INDIVIDUAL asset quality with three hero assets, then use them as reusable
templates (with variation) in the city. Previous city saved as
blender/out/city_pass03_final.blend; never overwrite it.

A. TAXI: a real detailed automobile, convincing in a close-up render: accurate automotive
   proportions, detailed body panels, realistic wheel geometry, tyre tread, rims, headlight
   housings, taillights, mirrors, door handles, wipers, glass reflections, interior seats
   and dashboard, licence plates, taxi signage, paint imperfections, realistic automotive
   materials.
B. BUILDING FACADE: one complete commercial architectural section: individual bricks and
   mortar, windows with real depth, frames and mullions, glass reflections, visible
   interior spaces, ledges, architectural trim, metal railings, drainage pipes, electrical
   conduits, ventilation equipment, exterior lights, signage, weathering, dirt
   accumulation, structural transitions. No flat facade texture standing in for geometry.
C. STREET CORNER: realistic pavement geometry, asphalt surface, curbs, manhole covers,
   drainage grates, road markings, traffic signals, streetlights, utility cabinets, fire
   hydrant, trash bins, street signs, bollards, small environmental objects, weathering and
   surface imperfections; real dimensions and correct placement.

Technical: actual Blender geometry, high-quality materials, proper UVs, realistic
lighting; efficient reusable meshes; every geometric detail must have a visual purpose;
judge by high-quality close-up renders.
Integration (after the assets pass inspection): into the existing city, as templates for
variations; no identical duplicates; do not replace the city.
Validation: close-up render of each asset; one render with all three in the city; compared
against the current project. More geometry is not success: visible realism is.
